About Phantom Depth Studio

Phantom Depth Studio is Afana Labs' dedicated game development division, founded with a singular vision: to create immersive, narrative-driven experiences that challenge players' perceptions of reality and self.

Currently operating as a solo operation with one dedicated team member, we focus on quality over quantity, crafting each game with meticulous attention to storytelling, psychological depth, and innovative gameplay mechanics.

Our Philosophy

  • Quality First: Every game is crafted with the same level of care and attention, regardless of scope
  • Narrative Depth: Stories that explore the human condition, reality, and consciousness
  • Innovation: Pushing boundaries of interactive storytelling and game design
  • Independence: Maintaining creative freedom while delivering exceptional experiences
